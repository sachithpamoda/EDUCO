AI Tool Reflection for Design Support

During the development of the AquaAction web application, Google Gemini AI was heavily utilized as an architectural co-pilot to streamline the design thinking process. I leveraged prompts to brainstorm and draft initial CSS structures for the "Dark Abyss" theme globally, as well as to quickly aggregate and structure technical SDG 06 statistical data efficiently.

Crucial Technical Adaptations

To comply with academic standards, AI outputs were never copied blindly. Instead, they were used as foundational blueprints which I manually adapted for the exact requirements of Student 1 role deliverables:

"The AI suggested a standard grid, which I manually modified into a 3x2 portrait grid to better fit my 300x400 source images."
"I manually implemented the Requirement C 'Targeted Text Scaling' logic to ensure the Navigation and Footer remain stable while main content enlarges."
"I used AI to assist in identifying and resolving Nu HTML Checker errors like empty headings and bad image attributes."
