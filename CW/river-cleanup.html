River Clean-Up Brigade

Every weekend, dedicated volunteers come together to restore our urban waterways. The River Clean-Up Brigade removes plastic waste, monitors water quality, and educates the public — one river at a time.

About the Programme

The River Clean-Up Brigade was founded in 2019 in response to alarming levels of plastic pollution discovered in local rivers. What began as a small group of 12 volunteers now coordinates clean-up events across 14 urban sites every weekend, removing an average of 400 kg of waste per month.

The initiative is open to anyone aged 14 and over. No prior experience is needed — just a willingness to roll up your sleeves and make a difference.

Our Impact

400 kg of waste removed monthlyOur volunteers have collectively cleared over 28 tonnes of plastic, glass, and metal from local waterways since 2019.
  • 14 active clean-up sites across the urban area
  • Partnered with 3 local councils and 2 environmental NGOs
  • Water quality monitoring data shared with the Environment Agency
  • Over 5,000 volunteer sessions logged since launch

How to Join

Joining is simple. Register your interest using the feedback form below, and our coordinator will add you to the next available weekend session in your area. All equipment — litter pickers, gloves, and bags — is provided on the day.

  • Register via the Feedback form (select "River Clean-Up Brigade")
  • Attend a 10-minute safety briefing at the start of each session
  • Spend 2–3 hours cleaning your assigned river section
  • Help log waste data for our environmental impact report
